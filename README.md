# Steady

**Small steps, most days.** A free, private Android app for daily habits, gentle reminders, mood check-ins and getting the tough stuff done.

👉 **[Download Steady for Android](https://jaidoonser.github.io/steady/)**

- Reminders that keep nudging (kindly) until it's done
- "Get it done" help for tasks you're dreading: name it, shrink it, do 10 minutes
- "Work through it" for rough days: say the problem out loud, narrow it, find one thing to do, then breathe
- Mood check-ins, mantras, guided breathing, journal
- A rewards ladder for full weeks
- Support lines for your country

**Private by design:** no account, no ads, no tracking, no server. Everything stays on your phone.

Steady is a self-help tool, not medical advice or treatment. If you're in crisis, call your local emergency number or find a helpline at [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com).

## About the code

The app screen is `app/src/main/assets/index.html` (plain HTML/JS). The Kotlin in `app/src/main/java` handles reminders, the camera, voice recording, backups and support-line links.

Official APKs are signed privately and published on the [Releases](https://github.com/jaidoonser/steady/releases) page. The signing key isn't in this repo.
